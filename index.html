    How a project runs

    Clear steps and regular check-ins, so you always know where your app stands and what happens next.

    1. 01

      Discover

      We go through your idea, your users, your business goals and any technical limits, so the scope is clear before a line of code is written.

    2. 02

      Plan

      Architecture, platform choice, screen flows, APIs and milestones, agreed up front and mapped to your budget.

    3. 03

      Build

      Features land in small, testable pieces with clean, maintainable code you can hand to another developer later.

    4. 04

      Test

      Real-device testing, performance tuning and bug fixing across screen sizes and OS versions.

    5. 05

      Launch

      Store listings, review submission and release on Google Play and the App Store.

    6. 06

      Improve

      Maintenance, crash monitoring, analytics and the next round of features once real users are in.
